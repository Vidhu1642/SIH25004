About Our Project

The Cattle vs Buffalo Classifier is an AI-powered tool designed to distinguish between cattle and buffalo using computer vision and deep learning. It supports farmers, researchers, and the livestock industry in making informed decisions.

✨ Why We Built This

Livestock identification is crucial in rural India for breeding, healthcare, and productivity improvement. This tool provides quick and accurate classification, helping reduce manual errors and saving time.

⚙️ How It Works

Users upload an animal image, and our trained deep learning model predicts whether it is a Cattle or Buffalo. The system is powered by datasets from reliable sources such as ICAR-NBAGR.
