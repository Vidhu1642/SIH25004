About Our Project

The Cattle & Buffalo Breed Recognizer is an AI-powered tool designed to recognize specific breeds of cattle and buffalo using computer vision and deep learning. It helps farmers, researchers, and the livestock industry make informed decisions quickly and accurately.

Why We Built This

Livestock identification is crucial in rural India for breeding, healthcare, and productivity improvement. This tool provides quick and accurate classification, reducing manual errors and saving time.

How It Works

Users upload an image, and our trained deep learning model predicts the specific breed of the cattle or buffalo. Powered by datasets from reliable sources like ICAR-NBAGR.
